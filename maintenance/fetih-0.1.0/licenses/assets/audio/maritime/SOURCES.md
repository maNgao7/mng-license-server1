# Türkiye maritime audio

The four mono 44.1 kHz Vorbis clips use CC0 field recordings. They were edited from the authors’ public Freesound previews. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

| File | Recording and edit |
| --- | --- |
| `ferry-horn.ogg` | nathanvladimirovich’s Staten Island Ferry horn, 0.14–3.30 s, with softened attack and tail. [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/nathanvladimirovich/sounds/261847/) |
| `boat-wash.ogg` | nomadas’ bow recording from a Loire river boat, 15–24 s, with a 0.62 s loop crossfade. [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/nomadas/sounds/690162/) |
| `ship-diesel.ogg` | AugustSandberg’s diesel recording, 7–15 s, filtered and looped with a 0.30 s crossfade. [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/AugustSandberg/sounds/237292/) |
| `coastal-salvo.ogg` | Fission9’s Distant Thunder 3. The opening rumble and transient were filtered, layered into two pulses and given a short echo. [Freesound](https://freesound.org/people/Fission9/sounds/581124/) |
