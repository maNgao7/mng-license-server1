# Ring widget artwork

Built-in image generation, transparent alpha; accepted cartoon variant.

Source: cartoon-boxers-v1.png. Runtime encoding: cartoon-boxers-v1.webp (1024×512, alpha preserved).
The profile photographs, team names, gifts and health are live HTML elements, not baked into this illustration.

Prompt: two matching cute cartoon boxing glove mascots on a transparent 2:1 atlas, blue on the left and red on the right, bold dark outlines and simple cel shading; two raised gloves on curved arms connected to a small athletic jersey, no human anatomy or head, empty space for large circular profile photos, no text or fixed gift icons.
